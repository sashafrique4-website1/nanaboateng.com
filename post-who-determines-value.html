Who Determines Value?

Growth isn't only about convincing the world that your idea has value. It's about having the humility to let your customers define it for you.

By Nana Boateng · 2025 · 3 min read

During the Q&A session at the just-ended Campus Exchange, where I spoke on the topic “The Entrepreneurial Mindset: From Idea to Impact,” a student asked a profound question.

“Sir, who actually determines value? Is it the business owner who creates the product, or the customer who buys it?”

That's a brilliant question, I responded.

Earlier in my presentation, I had shared a foundational mathematical formula:

Value = Benefits − Cost

As founders and growth leaders, we often look at this formula from our own perspective. We build a product, calculate our margins, list the features, and proudly declare its worth.

But I looked at that student and told them the unfiltered truth:

The moment your idea hits the market, you do not get to decide its value. The customer does. If the friction, time, or money (Cost) of using your product outweighs the functional or emotional payoff (Benefits) in their eyes, your product or service is not valuable.

To them, Impact only happens when your perception of benefit aligns perfectly with their reality.

To Leaders who are still confused about who determines value, I believe this is a call to action.

For growth isn't only about convincing the world that your idea has value. It's about having the humility to let your customers define it for you.
