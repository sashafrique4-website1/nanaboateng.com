Small Actions, Big Impact

Growth is also about becoming more conscious of the impact of the decisions we make.

By Nana Boateng · 2025 · 3 min read

Last Wednesday, I attended the MTN Ghana Sustainability Programme themed “Small Actions, Big Impact.”

I left the programme thinking about one small change we had made at Sash Afrique Ltd.

We used to package our sashes in rubber. As the business grew, we decided to move to box packaging.

At the time, the decision was mainly about improving our packaging and giving our Achievers a better experience. I did not think about sustainability when we made the change.

But sitting at the programme and listening to the conversations around sustainability and the SDGs made me look at that decision differently. It reminded me that sustainability can find its way into a business through the small choices we make every day.

The materials we use. The way we package our products. The things we consume. The things we throw away.

As SMEs, we may not be able to tackle every sustainability challenge, but we can start by looking closely at our own operations and asking:

What can we do differently?

That small change from rubber to boxes may seem insignificant on its own. But when thousands of businesses begin making thoughtful changes within their own operations, the collective impact can become significant.

That was my biggest takeaway from “Small Actions, Big Impact.”

Growth is also about becoming more conscious of the impact of the decisions we make.
