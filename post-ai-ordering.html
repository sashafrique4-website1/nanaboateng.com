What AI Taught Us About Serving Our Customers Better

AI should become part of how we think about improving our businesses. The starting point should always be the business problem.

By Nana Boateng · 2025 · 3 min read

I have been thinking a lot about how SMEs can use AI to improve the way they work.

At Sash Afrique Ltd, we had a challenge. Our Achievers had questions throughout the ordering process. What pattern should they choose? How do they personalize their sash? What information do they need to provide?

Our team was spending a lot of time answering these questions, and we knew there had to be a better way.

So we decided to ask our achievers.

We conducted a survey to understand their experience and where they needed the most help. The feedback showed us something important. Our achievers wanted a simpler way to move through the ordering process. They wanted guidance as they made their choices.

That led us to introduce an AI assisted ordering guide on our website.

This experience has reinforced something I believe SMEs should pay attention to.

AI can help a small business save time, improve customer experience and allow a small team to handle more work.

The starting point, however, should always be the business problem.

Look at what is taking too much time. Look at where your customers are getting stuck. Look at the repetitive tasks your team handles every day.

Then ask yourself: Can technology help us do this better?

That is where I think the real opportunity for SMEs lies.

AI should become part of how we think about improving our businesses.
